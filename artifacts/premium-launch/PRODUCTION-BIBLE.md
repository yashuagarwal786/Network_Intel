## A. Final narrative

1. A criminal network does not arrive as a diagram; it appears as fragments scattered across unrelated records.
2. As records multiply, the search space expands and manual correlation becomes the investigation bottleneck.
3. Network Intel enters only after the need is clear: turn fragmented records into a connected, inspectable investigation.
4. Operation Trinetra becomes the single thread through the product: records become entities, uncertain identities remain reviewable, and relationships form a graph.
5. The hero reveal traces the real six-hop Rahul Sharma–Vikram Singh association one sourced step at a time, with the Signal Corridor reinforcing the chain.
6. The film immediately answers the trust question by opening exact evidence and separating evidence, inference, and human judgment.
7. The investigator requests more evidence; the audit trail proves that the product assists review rather than declaring guilt.
8. The scattered opening fragments resolve into a connected, evidence-backed view, ending on Network Intel and SIH 26189.

## C. Complete voiceover script

A criminal network rarely appears in a single file.

One name may sit in a complaint. A phone in a call record. An account in a transaction. A location somewhere else.

The evidence may already exist. The connection may still be buried. And as records multiply, the search space grows faster than any manual review.

What if those fragments could become one connected investigation?

Network Intel.

It processes supported structured records and controlled report text, turning isolated mentions into entities investigators can work with—people, phones, accounts, vehicles and locations.

Because the same person can appear differently across records, Network Intel proposes possible identity matches. Evidence supports the comparison. Uncertainty stays with the investigator.

Relationships once scattered across files become part of one investigation graph. People, devices and accounts can now be explored in context, without losing the records that support them.

Now ask a question that would otherwise require record-by-record searching: how are Rahul Sharma and Vikram Singh connected?

Network Intel runs a bounded graph search. One sourced step at a time, a six-hop association emerges—through P101, P204, Amit Verma, A17 and A31.

But an investigation cannot depend on a black box. Every important connection must remain inspectable.

Here, the analytical lead returns to the transaction file, exact row, timestamp, verification status and original excerpt.

The system surfaces a connection. Evidence supports it. The investigator remains the final authority—able to confirm, reject, defer, or request more evidence, with the decision preserved for review.

Less manual cross-referencing. Non-obvious paths made visible. Reviewable leads with their evidence still attached.

The evidence may already be there.

Network Intel helps investigators see how it connects.
